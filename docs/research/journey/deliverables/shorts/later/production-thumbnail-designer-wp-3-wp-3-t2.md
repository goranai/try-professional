# Thumbnail briefs for 3 AI-tool Shorts

By production/thumbnail-designer. Status: approved.

Three thumbnail briefs, one per script in script order, each with headline (4 words), layout, visual elements, colors/contrast and a phone-size check step, plus a third-party asset and license section. Phone legibility is untested, CTR is unverified, and the Script 3 app is not yet chosen.

## Outputs
- BRIEF 1 (Script 1, Google vs Perplexity). Headline: WHICH ANSWER HELPS MORE? (4 words; matches hook 'Which answer helps more?'). Layout: vertical 9:16; headline in top third on 2 lines; below it a split screen with a vertical divider; keep text in the central safe area away from Shorts UI overlap. Visual elements: left half an empty search box with the text label 'Google', right half an empty search box with the text label 'Perplexity', one question mark between them. No results, no winner, no checkmark (no real recording exists yet). Colors/contrast: near-black background, white headline with thick dark outline; the two halves in two clearly different mid-tone colors that do not imitate vendor brand colors. A person checks the real contrast. Phone-size check (UNTESTED, for a person): view the exported image at about 120 px wide or on a real phone at thumbnail size; confirm the headline reads in about 1 second, the two halves are distinguishable, and nothing is covered by the Shorts UI. If any fails, enlarge or shorten the text.
- BRIEF 2 (Script 2, Cursor app from one sentence). Headline: APP FROM ONE SENTENCE? (4 words; matches hook 'Can Cursor build an app from one sentence?' and the script's on-screen headline). Layout: vertical 9:16; headline in top third on 2 lines ('APP FROM' / 'ONE SENTENCE?'); lower two-thirds show a simplified editor window containing one typed line. Visual elements: a generic code-editor mock-up (not a Cursor screenshot) with a one-line prompt and a text cursor; optionally a small error-style red label reading 'ERRORS INCLUDED', which the script does show. Text name 'Cursor' in small plain text instead of a logo. No result of the app is shown, since it is not yet recorded. Colors/contrast: dark editor panel, white headline with dark outline, one accent color (yellow or cyan) for the key words 'ONE SENTENCE'. Phone-size check (UNTESTED, for a person): view at about 120 px wide or on a real phone; confirm the headline and the 'ERRORS INCLUDED' label are legible and the editor mock-up does not clutter the text. Drop the label if it is unreadable.
- BRIEF 3 (Script 3, AI note-taking timed test). Headline: HOW LONG TO SUMMARIZE? (4 words; matches the hook 'How long does an AI note app take to summarize my notes?' and makes no time-saved claim). Layout: vertical 9:16; headline in top third on 2 lines; a large stopwatch graphic in the center; a small stack of note pages on one side. Visual elements: stopwatch showing '00:00' or a blank face, NOT a time, because no run has been measured. A generic notes-page icon. No app name, no app logo, no app UI: the app to feature has NOT been chosen. App choice is PENDING a person's decision; once chosen, the brief must be updated, and the choice and its terms checked. Colors/contrast: dark background, white headline with dark outline, stopwatch in a bright accent color. Phone-size check (UNTESTED, for a person): view at about 120 px wide or on a real phone; confirm the headline and stopwatch are readable in about 1 second. After the real run, only put a time on the thumbnail if it was measured and matches the video.
- THIRD-PARTY ASSETS AND LICENSE STATUS (all UNVERIFIED). Planned design uses only generic, self-made shapes: search boxes, editor mock-up, stopwatch, notes icon, and plain-text tool names. Still to check by a person: (1) Plain-text names 'Google', 'Perplexity', 'Cursor': trademark and vendor brand guidelines on nominative use; status unverified. (2) No vendor logos or UI screenshots are in the briefs; if a person wants to add any (Google, Perplexity, Cursor, the Script 3 app), check each vendor's brand and terms pages first; status unverified. (3) Any stock icons, fonts or images used by the designer: license and commercial-use terms must be confirmed; none are chosen yet. (4) Script 3 app: not chosen; once picked, its logo/UI/terms are unverified. (5) Any later real screenshot from the recordings needs the same terms check and the YouTube AI-disclosure rules check.
