THREE SHORTS SCRIPTS: AI TOOLS

Square brackets [ ] are placeholders to be filled from a real, creator-recorded demo. No winner is assumed. Duration basis for all three: spoken words / 150 words per minute. NOT timed aloud. Bracketed fill-ins are not counted and add a few seconds, so each should still land well under 60s.

=== SCRIPT 1 ===
TOPIC: Google vs Perplexity, same question side by side
APPLIED LEARNINGS: 'honest demo' (show real results, assume no winner) and 'consistent structure' (about 4s hook, body, comment CTA).
HOOK (~4s): "Same question, Google and Perplexity. Which answer helps more?"
BODY: "I'm asking both the exact same question: [question]. Google on the left, Perplexity on the right. Google gave me [what it showed]. Perplexity gave me [what it showed]. I judged them on [criteria decided BEFORE recording]. What I saw: [honest observation, including anything wrong or unclear]. That's one question, so it proves nothing on its own. A different question could go differently."
CTA (comment): "Comment a question you want me to test next, and follow for the next side by side."
VISUALS: 1) Split screen, both search boxes empty, text 'SAME QUESTION'. 2) Question typed into both. 3) Unedited screen recording of each result. 4) Criteria as 2-3 words on screen. 5) End card 'COMMENT A QUESTION'.
ESTIMATED DURATION: about 95 spoken words, about 38s at 150 wpm; not timed aloud.
UNVERIFIED / TO CHECK (a person): (a) performance of any earlier Short: check YouTube Studio and confirm whether anything was published; (b) trend status of this topic: check Google Trends/YouTube search for the last 7 days; (c) vendor features, pricing and terms for Google and Perplexity: no feature, price or free-tier claim is made in this script, check vendor pages before adding any; (d) permission/terms for showing logos and UI on screen; (e) YouTube AI-disclosure rules (the demo shows AI-generated output); (f) the result lines are placeholders until a real recording exists.

=== SCRIPT 2 ===
TOPIC: Cursor builds a tiny app from one sentence, errors included
APPLIED LEARNINGS: 'honest demo' (show the full attempt, errors and fixes) and 'question-style hooks' (hook is a question that matches the on-screen headline).
HOOK (~4s): "Can Cursor build an app from one sentence?"
BODY: "I typed one sentence: [exact prompt]. No edits. Here's the first try. [Show what happened, including the first error.] I'm keeping every error and every fix I asked for in this video. After [number] tries, the result was [working / partly working / not working]. Time on screen: [measured]. That's one prompt, one attempt, so it's not a verdict on the tool."
CTA (comment): "Want a different prompt tested? Comment one, and follow for more."
VISUALS: 1) Headline 'APP FROM ONE SENTENCE?' over the empty editor. 2) Prompt typed in full view. 3) Screen recording of the first run and the real error message, unhidden. 4) Fix attempts with a try counter. 5) Final state of the app, as it really ended. 6) End card 'COMMENT A PROMPT'.
ESTIMATED DURATION: about 85 spoken words, about 34s at 150 wpm; not timed aloud.
UNVERIFIED / TO CHECK (a person): (a) performance of earlier Shorts: YouTube Studio, confirm what was published; (b) trend status of this topic and whether the 'vibe coding' audience is current: check Google Trends/YouTube/X/Reddit for the last 7 days; (c) Cursor's current features, pricing, free-tier limits, model access and terms: none are claimed here, check the vendor site, changelog and pricing page; (d) logo/UI on-screen use and any terms on showing generated output; (e) YouTube AI-disclosure rules; (f) all bracketed results must come from a real recording, and the 'working' status must be tested.

=== SCRIPT 3 ===
TOPIC: AI note-taking app, a timed test of one note-to-summary task
APPLIED LEARNING: 'no unmeasured claims' (every number on screen is measured; no unmeasured time-saving wording).
HOOK (~4s): "How long does an AI note app take to summarize my notes?"
BODY: "One task, one stopwatch. I'm giving [app name] about [number] words of messy notes from [meeting or lecture type]. Start. [What I did, step by step.] The summary took [measured time]. Then I checked it against my notes. It got [what it caught] and missed [what it missed]. That's one run on one app, so don't read it as a ranking."
CTA (comment): "Which app should I time next? Comment it, and follow."
VISUALS: 1) Visible stopwatch starts on screen. 2) Notes pasted or uploaded, shown in full. 3) Summary appearing, stopwatch still running. 4) Stopwatch frozen at the measured time. 5) Split screen: summary vs original notes with misses highlighted. 6) End card 'WHICH APP NEXT?'.
ESTIMATED DURATION: about 85 spoken words, about 34s at 150 wpm; not timed aloud.
UNVERIFIED / TO CHECK (a person): (a) performance of earlier Shorts, including last week's script title and the separately chosen note-taking title, which had no keyword data or A/B test: YouTube Studio; (b) trend status of the topic: check Google Trends/YouTube for the last 7 days; (c) the app to feature has NOT been chosen: a person must choose one app and check it; (d) that app's features, pricing, free-tier limits, release dates and terms: none are claimed, check the vendor pages; (e) logo/UI on-screen use; (f) YouTube AI-disclosure rules; (g) the time and quality notes must come from a real timed run, not estimated.
