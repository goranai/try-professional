# Thumbnail briefs for 5 Shorts

By production/thumbnail-designer. Status: approved.

Five thumbnail briefs, one per Short in script order, all vertical 9:16. Headlines are 5 words or fewer and match each hook. None claims new, trending, pricing or time saved. Briefs only, no images produced. Logo/UI permissions and real contrast ratios are unverified.

## Outputs
- COMMON RULES: 9:16 (1080x1920). Keep focal element and text in the central safe area, away from the bottom 20% and right edge where Shorts UI overlays sit. Bold sans-serif headline, max 2 lines, large enough to read at about 3 cm wide. Dark background with white or yellow headline plus outline. Use only screenshots from the creator's own real recording. No NEW, TRENDING, FREE, price or ranking badges.
- BRIEF 1 (Script 1, Google vs Perplexity). Layout: 9:16, split top/bottom with a diagonal divider. Focal element: the same typed question in both halves, cropped on the answer areas, from the real recording. Headline: 'GOOGLE vs PERPLEXITY?' (3 words), matching the hook 'Same question, two tools'. Colour/contrast: dark neutral background, one accent per half (blue, teal), white headline with dark outline. Phone-size check: both halves and the headline must be distinguishable at thumbnail size; if answers are unreadable, crop to blocks and do not imply a result. Logo/UI: Google and Perplexity names, logos and UI are third-party; check each vendor's brand and terms pages, and use plain text names if unclear. UNVERIFIED: permission to show either product; the creator's actual results.
- BRIEF 2 (Script 2, PDF to podcast). Layout: 9:16, document page at top, headphones or waveform below, joined by an arrow. Focal element: the creator's real screenshot of the upload or audio option next to the document icon. Headline: 'PDF TO PODCAST?' (3 words), a question because the hook is 'Let's try turning a PDF into something you can listen to'. Colour/contrast: deep navy background, warm yellow headline, white icons. Phone-size check: the headline and the two icons must read at thumbnail size; the UI screenshot is decorative and need not be legible. Logo/UI: NotebookLM name, logo and UI are Google third-party material; check Google's terms and brand rules for on-screen use. Use generic icons rather than the logo if unclear. UNVERIFIED: that NotebookLM currently offers the audio option; the quality of the creator's actual output. Do not show an uploaded document the creator has no right to use.
- BRIEF 3 (Script 3, Cursor). Layout: 9:16, a short one-sentence prompt in a text box at top, and the creator's real finished or attempted app below. Focal element: the prompt box with the exact prompt used in the video. Headline: 'APP FROM ONE SENTENCE?' (4 words), a question that matches 'Can I build a tiny app just by describing it? Let's find out.' Colour/contrast: near-black background, bright green or cyan headline, white prompt text. Phone-size check: the headline must be readable at thumbnail size; prompt text must be large, or cut to a few words. If the app broke in the recording, do not show a polished result; show the real state or leave the app out. Logo/UI: Cursor name, logo and editor UI are third-party; check Cursor's terms and brand guidelines, and crop the UI or use a plain text name if unclear. UNVERIFIED: whether the chosen app builds; the creator's actual results.
- BRIEF 4 (Script 4, ElevenLabs). Layout: 9:16, a text snippet or caption at top turning into a sound waveform below. Focal element: the waveform with a speaker or headphones icon, optionally the creator's real screenshot of the text input. Headline: 'TEXT TO VOICE: HONEST TEST' (5 words), matching 'turn plain text into a voiceover and judge it honestly'. Colour/contrast: dark purple or charcoal background, white and yellow headline, one bright waveform colour. Phone-size check: the waveform and the headline must read at thumbnail size; keep it to two text lines. Do not use words like 'realistic', 'perfect' or 'human' unless the creator's recording supports them. Logo/UI: ElevenLabs name, logo and UI are third-party; check its terms and brand rules, and whether the voice used may be shown. UNVERIFIED: commercial-use terms, YouTube AI-disclosure rules, and the creator's actual audio quality.
- BRIEF 5 (Script 5, prompts). Layout: 9:16, three numbered cards (1, 2, 3) stacked vertically, each with a few words of the prompt topic (summary, checklist, questions). Focal element: the three numbered cards. Headline: '3 PROMPTS TO TRY' (4 words). It matches 'Three prompts to try for your own busy work'. Do not use 'an hour' or any time-saved figure unless the creator times the demos and the video states the measured result. Colour/contrast: dark background, three card colours that differ in brightness, white numerals and text, yellow headline. Phone-size check: the numerals 1-3 and the headline must be readable at thumbnail size; card text may be tiny or abstract. Logo/UI: avoid Claude and ChatGPT logos and UI by default; if shown, check Anthropic's and OpenAI's terms and brand guidelines. Show no private data in any screenshot. UNVERIFIED: which assistant and model version is shown; the creator's actual time saved.

## Open issues
- Nobody has checked whether each vendor allows showing its name, logo or UI on screen. A person should check this on each vendor's terms and brand pages.
- Contrast ratios and phone-size legibility were specified but not tested on an image. Check these on a real phone once the thumbnails are made.
- Thumbnails must reflect what the creator actually records. If a demo fails or differs, update the focal element and headline.
- Brief 5 headline avoids the hour claim; the script title still says 'Might Save You an Hour', so the producer should align it unless the creator times it.
