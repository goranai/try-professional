PROPOSAL: Online Pre-Orders for [BAKERY NAME]
Prepared by Neighborhood Web Studio | [date]

WHAT YOU ASKED FOR
You would like customers to be able to pre-order from your bakery online. This proposal describes a simple website that does that. We have not yet seen your menu or how orders reach you today, so anything we assume is listed under Assumptions.

WHAT THE WEBSITE WILL DO FOR YOU
- Let customers choose items, pick a pickup day and time, and send you an order at any hour.
- Show your menu, opening hours, address and contact details in one place.
- Work well on phones.
- Pre-orders can mean fewer missed calls, fewer mix-ups and a clearer idea of what to bake each day.

SCOPE: WHAT IS INCLUDED
1. A small website: Home, Menu, Order, About and Contact pages.
2. How pre-ordering works: the customer picks items and quantities, chooses a pickup date and time, enters their name and phone number, and sends the order. You receive it by email and in a simple list. You confirm it, and the customer is told it is confirmed.
3. Pickup only, with payment when the customer collects (proposed, to be confirmed).
4. A setting to close orders for a day after a cut-off time you choose (proposed, to be confirmed).
5. Short training so you can update menu items and prices yourself (proposed, to be confirmed).

NOT INCLUDED
Delivery, online card payment, logo or brand design, professional photography and ongoing marketing. Writing menu descriptions and editing photos could be offered for an extra fee (proposed, to be confirmed). Domain and hosting costs are not yet priced.
